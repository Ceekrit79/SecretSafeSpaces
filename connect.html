Safe Space Guidelines

How Our 1-on-1 Rooms Work

Our Support Confidants are compassionate peer listeners who hold space for everyday stress, burnout, and life's heavy moments.

Whenever you feel ready, submit your anonymous alias and topic to be paired with an available listener.

  • 100% Anonymous: No real names, emails, or personal details required.
  • Judgment-Free Zone: Share at your own comfort level without pressure.
  • Peer-to-Peer Support: Built for everyday venting and emotional decompression.
  • Take your time: There is no rush to type. Pause, breathe, or step away whenever you need.
  • Share at your comfort level: Use your chosen alias and share only what feels safe today.
  • Mutual Kindness: Keep conversations respectful, supportive, and free of judgment.
